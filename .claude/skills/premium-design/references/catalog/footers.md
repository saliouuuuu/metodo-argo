## Variant 1: Editorial Manifesto Footer

**Best for:** Premium brands where the footer is a final spread, not a utility band.

**Key features:**
- **Dark warm background** (#141210) with warm terracotta accent glow bottom-left
- **Top manifesto row** — massive 2-line serif display "statement" (up to 8rem), italic second line. Mask reveal on scroll.
- **CTA pair** — solid off-white button + two-tone underline email link
- **3 sitemap columns** — italic serif headings ("the practice", "engagements", "elsewhere") + uppercase micro-label links with terracotta underline-slide hover
- **4th column: Newsletter** — minimalist underline input, arrow submit button, terracotta focus underline
- **Bottom row** — large italic wordmark + location-year meta + uppercase legal links with hairline separators

**Structure:** `heading` (italic serif) + `links: [{label, href}]` per column.

See: `components/footer-variant-1.tsx`
