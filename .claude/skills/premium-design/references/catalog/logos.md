## Variant 1: Editorial Client Ledger

**Best for:** Premium studios listing clients, creative agencies, luxury service providers — a social proof section that reads like a ledger, not a logo band.

**Key features:**
- **NO logo images** — client names in italic serif display (2.5rem)
- **Vertical list layout** — 12-col grid per row: number (1) + name (5) + discipline (3) + year (2) + location (1)
- **Hover interactions** — background gradient wash fades in from left, number shifts 6px right, location opacity jumps
- **Scroll stagger** — 50ms delay per row
- **Typography hierarchy** — name (serif 300), discipline (uppercase micro), year (serif italic), location (serif italic)

**Data shape:** `{ name, year, discipline, location }`

**Why no logos:** Premium sites often use typography because (a) consistent logo treatment is hard to achieve with mixed-brand marks, (b) typography communicates curation/discernment, (c) it works whether you have 5 or 50 clients.
